# CoDo Brand Foundation

## Positioning

CoDo is the dependable AI partner people can hand work to. It is not defined by a profession, a model, or a single workflow. The product earns trust by understanding the goal, taking useful action, and showing what happened.

## Slogan

**Ask less. Get more done.**

The line is intentionally plain. It distinguishes CoDo from assistants that create more conversation than progress, without promising that every request can be completed autonomously.

## Brand Character

- Calmly capable: confident without hype or theatrics.
- Helpful, not subservient: works alongside the user and takes ownership of clear tasks.
- Direct: uses short, concrete language and explains limits plainly.
- Broad but grounded: welcomes any profession while only claiming work the product can actually perform.
- Clear and composed: neutral surfaces keep attention on the task; blue signals trust, focus, and action.

## Voice

Prefer: "I can take that on", "Here is what I completed", "I need your approval to continue".

Avoid: mystical AI language, exaggerated autonomy, developer-only terminology in general surfaces, and filler such as "revolutionize" or "unlock".

## Visual System

- Primary accent: CoDo Blue `#0056FE`, sampled from the approved logo artwork
- Canvas: cool neutral `#F4F5F6`
- Primary text: neutral ink, never pure black
- Shape: compact 8-14px radii with stable rectangular controls
- Mark: a connected `C` and `D`, representing a request crossing into action

The logo stays flat and geometric at product sizes. Lighting, 3D, glow, and illustrative treatments belong to campaign artwork, not the core mark.
